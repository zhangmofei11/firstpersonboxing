🥊 TOP CONTENDERS

💀

"The Hurricane" Jackson

Lightweight Contender #1

Aggressive pressure fighter. Comes forward non-stop with volume punching. Watch: Overhand right after slipping.

🦅

"Steel Fist" Thompson

Welterweight Contender #1

One-punch knockout power. Patient fighter who waits for the perfect shot. Watch: Right hook counter.

👻

"The Phantom" Rodriguez

Bantamweight Contender #1

Elusive counterpuncher with exceptional reflexes. Difficult to hit cleanly. Watch: Lead hook off the jab.

🐂

"El Toro" Garcia

Strawweight Champion

Former champion with highoctane style. Aggressive but cardio-dependent. Watch: Second wind if you survive round 4.

⭐ RISING STARS

Heavy-handed prospect from Brazil. Looking for a title shot. Watch the left hook.

"Knuckles" Chen

Former amateur champion with slick boxing skills. Recovering from early career loss.

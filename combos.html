PUNCH COMBOS

Master these combinations to dominate the ring

📗 BASIC COMBOS

Jab-Cross

Jab → Cross

The foundation of all boxing. Use to measure distance and set up power shots.

Jab-Jab-Cross

Jab → Jab → Cross

More commitment than single jab. Dulls opponent's reaction to the jab.

Jab-Body-Right Hook

Jab → Body → Hook

Classic combination. Body shot drops hands, hook lands clean.

Rope-A-Rope

Hook → Hook → Cross

Double hook to the head, then a right cross. Devastating when it lands.

📙 INTERMEDIATE COMBOS

Check Hook

Slip → Hook

Counter off a slip. Land this when opponent overcommits.

Liver Shot Setup

Jab → Cross → Body-L

After stunning with head shots, the body shot becomes available.

Stun-Stun-Finish

Jab → Jab → Uppercut

Build up stun, then uppercut catches them coming in.

Overhand-Jab

Overhand → Jab

After overhand lands, the stunned opponent can't avoid the jab.

📕 ADVANCED KNOCKOUT COMBOS

4-Punch flurry

Jab → Cross → Hook → Hook

End with a decision: double jab or continue with cross.

Rice Bowl Combo

Jab → Jab → Cross → Body → Hook

5-punch sequence. Stuns, hurts body, then hooks to finish.

Floyd Special

Jab → Jab → Jab → Cross

Rapid-fire jab barrage, then a straight right. Hard to defend.

Rope-A-Fool

Jab → Cross → Hook → Hook → Cross

6-punch flurry. Finishes with a fight-ending straight right.

AMATEUR CAREER

  • • Fight 4: vs Frank "The Tank" Wilson

Flyweight Division

Move up to Flyweight. Face faster, more technical opponents.

Featherweight Division

WORLD CHAMPION

🏋️ TRAINING TIPS

Before Major Fights

Focus on speed drills and sparring. Rest 2 days before the fight.

Building Fundamentals

Spend time on heavy bag and footwork drills. Form is more important than power.

Recovery Training

After losses, focus on cardio and defensive techniques.

Skill Points Priority

Jab > Footwork > Defense > Power. This progression wins fights.

About the Game

First Person Boxing delivers an immersive boxing experience from a first-person perspective. Experience the intensity of professional boxing with realistic physics, authentic punch mechanics, and strategic footwork. Train your fighter, study opponents, and climb through the ranks from local amateur bouts to world championship fights.

💡 BEGINNER TIPS

Master the Jab

The jab is your most important tool. Use it to measure distance, set up combinations, and keep aggressive opponents at bay.

Footwork Matters

Stay on your toes, pivot properly, and maintain good balance. Good footwork is the foundation of effective boxing.

Study Your Opponent

Watch replays of previous fights. Learn patterns in their offense and exploit their weaknesses.
